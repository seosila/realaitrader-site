About

Real AI Trader is an independent publication covering artificial intelligence, automated systems, financial technology and the evolving role of machine learning in market research.

We focus on AI in financial markets, algorithmic trading, automated trading systems, trading technology, crypto and AI, and practical educational research for traders and technology-minded investors.

Our editorial approach is educational and informational. We aim to explain how tools, systems and market technology are changing the trading landscape without presenting the content as personalized financial advice.

Real AI Trader does not provide individualized financial guidance, accounting recommendations or tailored investment advice. The content is designed to help readers understand technology, strategy concepts and market behavior in a broader informational context.
